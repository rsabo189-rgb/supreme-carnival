EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Pepsi Summer 2026 is a cultural platform celebrating the moments, music, and connections that define summer. We're owning the soundtrack to unforgettable experiences—fueling optimism, spontaneity, and the joy of being together.

OBJECTIVES

Drive brand love and cultural relevance with Gen Z and Millennials
Increase consumption in key summer occasions
Amplify music and creator partnerships to extend reach

KEY INSIGHTS

78% say summer is their favorite season for making memories
Music is the #1 driver of mood and connection in summer
People crave real moments, not staged perfection
